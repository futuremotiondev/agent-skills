# Motion Vocabulary Reference

## Subject Motion Verbs

### Highest Reliability — Micro Motion (always safe)
breathes, blinks, gazes, shifts weight, exhales, inhales, swallows, furrows brow, parts lips, squints, softens expression, tenses jaw, tilts chin, flares nostrils, twitches, settles, adjusts posture

### High Reliability — Moderate Motion
turns (head, body, slightly), lifts (hand, cup, object, chin), reaches, leans (forward, back, against), steps (forward, back, aside), rises, sits, nods, tilts head, brushes hair aside, extends hand, withdraws, touches (face, object), grips, releases, crosses arms, uncrosses, pivots, glances, looks up/down/away

### Moderate Reliability — Full-Body Motion
walks, strides, runs, dances, spins (slowly), crouches, kneels, stretches, gestures, bows, stumbles, sways, balances, rocks on heels, paces, circles, retreats, advances, lunges

### Lower Reliability — Complex Motion (warn user)
jumps, flips, catches, throws, fights, climbs, falls, collapses, rolls, slides, somersaults, cartwheels, vaults

## Environmental Motion Vocabulary

### Atmospheric Particles
dust motes drift through a shaft of light, embers float upward from below frame, snow falls gently through the scene, rain streaks diagonally across the frame, pollen drifts lazily on a current, ash settles from above, steam curls upward from a surface, mist rolls across the ground, fog creeps inward from the edges, bubbles rise through liquid, smoke wisps upward, sparks scatter and fade, condensation forms on glass, frost crystals spread across a surface, sand grains lift in a breeze

### Fabric and Hair
hair catches a gentle breeze, hair strand shifts across the forehead, coat hem ripples in the wind, curtains billow inward from an open window, dress fabric shifts with weight transfer, scarf trails behind in motion, flag snaps in a gust, sheets flutter on a line, ribbons curl and unwind, lace trembles, collar flutters, sleeve brushes against a surface, veil lifts, fringe sways

### Natural Elements
leaves rustle on branches, grass sways in waves, water ripples outward from a disturbance, waves lap against a shore/edge, branches bob gently, petals fall one by one, clouds drift across the sky, shadows crawl across a surface, puddle surface shivers, flowers nod in a breeze, reeds bend and straighten, moss sways underwater, icicles drip, bark creaks, roots shift

### Light and Reflection
light shifts gradually from warm to cool, shadows lengthen across the floor, neon sign flickers irregularly, candlelight dances across a wall, headlights sweep across the frame, golden hour deepens to amber, reflections ripple on a wet surface, lens flare drifts across the frame, sunbeams move through atmospheric haze, screen glow pulses, streetlight hums to life, dawn light creeps across the horizon, twilight blue settles over the scene, firelight throws dancing shadows, light leak bleeds across the edge

### Urban and Mechanical
traffic flows as blurred streaks in background, neon signs pulse with varying rhythm, smoke rises from a chimney or vent, clock hands move imperceptibly, escalator steps cycle, elevator indicator lights shift, ceiling fan rotates at low speed, turnstile arm swings, door slowly closes on a pneumatic hinge, blinds sway against an air current, paper flutters on a bulletin board, a phone screen illuminates then dims, windshield wipers sweep

### Water
steam rises from hot liquid, condensation drips down a glass, rain collects and runs down a window, a drop falls into still water creating rings, tide creeps in over sand, waterfall mist drifts, fountain spray catches light, ice melts and water beads form, coffee swirls when disturbed, tea diffuses color into hot water

## Pacing and Temporal Language

### Slow / Gradual (default for most prompts)
slowly, gently, imperceptibly, languidly, leisurely, unhurriedly, at a glacial pace, almost imperceptibly, with deliberate slowness, in no hurry, with measured patience, at a meditative pace

### Building / Transitioning
gradually, progressively, incrementally, building toward, shifting from...to, easing into, transitioning from...to, gaining momentum, growing in intensity, evolving from...into, accelerating from stillness, mounting

### Sustained / Continuous
continuously, steadily, rhythmically, pulsing, cycling, oscillating, maintaining, persisting, unwavering, in an endless loop, with metronomic consistency, tirelessly

### Sudden / Punctuated (use sparingly — Dynamic archetype only)
suddenly, sharply, abruptly, in an instant, snapping into, bursting, erupting, jolting, launching, without warning, in a flash, all at once

### Temporal Anchoring
"at first...then...finally," "beginning as...evolving into," "what starts as...becomes," "the moment stretches," "time seems to slow," "the scene breathes," "a beat passes before"

## Lens and Optical Language

### Depth of Field
shallow depth of field blurs the background into soft circles of light, deep focus keeps every plane razor-sharp, selective focus isolates the subject from a dreamy background, focus falls off dramatically beyond the subject, tilt-shift miniaturization effect on the environment

### Focus Behavior
rack focus pulls from foreground to background (or reverse), focus breathes gently in and out, subject snaps into sharp focus from soft, background sharpens as foreground dissolves, split diopter keeps two planes sharp simultaneously

### Lens Character
anamorphic lens flare streaks horizontally across highlights, vintage lens creates swirling bokeh in the corners, wide-angle distortion stretches the edges of frame, telephoto compression flattens the depth planes together, macro lens reveals texture invisible to the eye, soft pro-mist glow around highlights, chromatic aberration at high contrast edges

### Film and Sensor
subtle film grain dances across the image, 35mm film texture with organic noise, 16mm film warmth and heavy grain, digital noise in the shadows, sensor bloom around bright highlights, halation glow around light sources

## Mood Transition Language

For the Narrative archetype, describing mood shifts adds emotional depth:

### Tension → Release
"the held breath finally exhales," "shoulders drop as tension dissolves," "the tight frame relaxes outward," "rigidity softens into ease"

### Stillness → Motion
"from perfect stillness, a first movement breaks the calm," "the static scene trembles to life," "what was frozen begins to breathe," "the spell of stillness breaks"

### Familiar → Strange
"the ordinary scene shifts almost imperceptibly toward the uncanny," "reality subtly warps at the edges," "normal proportions begin to feel slightly wrong," "the comfortable becomes uncertain"

### Light → Dark (or reverse)
"warmth drains from the light, leaving cold blue," "shadows advance across the frame like a tide," "a golden warmth bleeds into the scene from the edges," "darkness retreats as morning arrives"

### Calm → Energy (for Dynamic archetype transitions)
"the calm surface ripples with the first hint of disturbance," "stillness shatters into motion," "potential energy converts to kinetic in an instant," "the coiled tension releases"
