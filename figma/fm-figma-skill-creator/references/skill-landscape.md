# Skill landscape

Every Figma-agent skill this repo knows about, one row each, with no cap. The table is factual: it records what a skill does, not whether it is good. `skill-idea-catalog.md` computes its gap grid from this table, and Workflow 0 checks it before pitching any concept so a card can say whether it is new or better than an existing row.

How the columns are filled:

- **Source** is `Community` (a Figma Community listing, including Figma's own help-center examples), `Qredence` (the MIT-licensed Qredence figma-agent collection), or `Futuremotion` (this repo).
- **Category** is one of the six Figma Community categories: Research and strategy, Generation, Brand, Design systems, Critique, Workflows.
- **Pattern** is the primary one of audit (checks against a stated rule and lists deviations), generator (creates new canvas content, documents, or code), critique (judgement-based review), synthesis (turns raw material into themes or summaries), workflow (a staged process, plan, migration, or routing step), and conventions (applies or grounds a standard).
- **Surface** is the primary one of components, variables, type, colour, layout, content, accessibility, motion, handoff, and documentation. Comments, stickies, and research notes count as content; prototype interactions count as motion; code output and Dev Mode preparation count as handoff; briefs, specs, guides, and reports count as documentation.
- **One-line purpose** is written fresh, under 20 words. A note in parentheses at its end records a merged duplicate or the evidence behind a row.
- **Notable idiom** is the one thing worth copying from the skill, or `—`.
- **Seen** is the date the skill entered this table.

Twelve Community listings published by Qredence are the same files as their Qredence rows and are merged into them. Ten Community rows come from skills named or quoted in the research report and research notes rather than from a captured body; the note at the end of each purpose line names its source, and says when the function is inferred from the name and recorded tools.

## Refresh

New rows come from running `fm-figma-design-skill-finder` (in this repo) for Community skills and from any skill the user shares. Append new rows and never rewrite or delete history; a skill that changes gets a new row with its new Seen date. Recompute the gap grid in `skill-idea-catalog.md` after each refresh.
